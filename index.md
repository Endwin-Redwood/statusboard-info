# StatusBoard

StatusBoard is a private family dashboard that runs on a home computer and is shown on a wall-mounted display. It shows the family's calendar, tasks, local weather, countdowns, photos and short messages on one screen.

## How it uses Google data

StatusBoard signs in to the owner's Google account with **read-only** access to:

- **Google Calendar**: to show upcoming events on the display.
- **Google Tasks**: to show open tasks on the display.

It never creates, changes or deletes calendar events or tasks.

StatusBoard is a personal, self-hosted app for one household. It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.
